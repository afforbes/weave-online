Pull your chair up to the fire. Each stitch is one swipe that traces the needle tip: push it into the loop, wrap the yarn, pull it through, slip it off. Your needles move as you draw.

Start at the dot and finish at the arrow. In a circle, everyone's finished rows join one shared blanket. 12 stitches make a row. The pattern changes every 4 rows, and neat stitches in a row stoke the fire.
